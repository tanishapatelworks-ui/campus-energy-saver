# Campus Energy Saver

### Student Energy Consumption & Saving Habits Study

Campus Energy Saver is an SDG-focused student research project that studies everyday electricity consumption and energy-saving habits among students.

The project focuses on identifying common energy-wasting behaviors, understanding students' awareness and willingness to change, and suggesting simple practical actions for more responsible electricity use.

## 🌱 SDG Alignment

- **SDG 7 – Affordable and Clean Energy**
- **SDG 13 – Climate Action**

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand students' everyday electricity-use habits.
- Identify common behaviors that may lead to unnecessary electricity consumption.
- Measure awareness of electricity-saving practices.
- Identify barriers that prevent students from saving energy.
- Understand students' willingness to adopt better energy-saving habits.
- Propose simple and practical energy-saving actions.

## 📊 Survey

An online survey was conducted using Google Forms.

- **Total responses:** 20
- **Target group:** Students and young learners
- **Survey duration:** Approximately 2–3 minutes
- **Focus:** Electricity usage, energy-saving habits, awareness, challenges, and willingness to change.

## 🔎 Key Findings

- **55%** of respondents use a laptop or computer for 4 or more hours per day.
- **55%** leave their charger plugged in at least sometimes after charging.
- **65%** leave lights on when they are not needed at least sometimes.
- **50%** leave fans running when nobody is using the room at least sometimes.
- **55%** leave electronic devices in standby mode at least sometimes.
- **80%** use power-saving features at least sometimes.
- Average electricity-awareness score was **3.1 out of 5**.
- **70%** of respondents were definitely or probably willing to change their energy-use habits.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

The project proposes a simple student-focused energy-saving approach:

1. Switch off unused lights and fans.
2. Unplug chargers when they are not required.
3. Switch off computers and other devices instead of leaving them in standby mode.
4. Use available laptop and device power-saving features.
5. Conduct awareness and reminder activities to encourage consistent energy-saving habits.
